Is my enquiry confidential?

Yes. Only the advisor who speaks with you sees your details, and we never share them without your consent.

Can I ask for someone else?

Of course. Many families call first. We will guide you on how to start the conversation with your loved one.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is needed. If you have medical records, bring them and our team will review them.

What if I only want to visit?

Book a tour at any time. You can meet the team, see the rooms and decide at your own pace.
