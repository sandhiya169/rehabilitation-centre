Our story

Our story

  1. 2008

    A small clinic with a big idea

    Stackly began as a ten-bed physiotherapy clinic on a quiet Chennai road, built on one belief: recovery needs time and kindness.

  2. 2013

    Mind and body, together

    We brought therapists and psychiatrists onto the same team, so physical and emotional healing could happen side by side.

  3. 2018

    The campus opens

    Gardens, a hydrotherapy pool and private rooms replaced the old clinic, and our addiction recovery program began.

  4. 2026

    Four thousand lives and counting

    Today a team of specialists supports residents and families from the first call to a full year of aftercare.

Our facility

A space built for healing

Every corner of our 12-acre campus is designed to lower stress, restore calm and give recovery the environment it deserves.

Quiet gardens

Landscaped walkways and open lawns where residents can reflect, breathe and recover at their own pace.

Hydrotherapy pool

A heated indoor pool used daily for physiotherapy, active reconditioning and supervised aquatic sessions.

Private rooms

Every resident has a private, light-filled room with garden views, ensuring rest and personal space throughout their stay.

Therapy studios

Dedicated spaces for one-to-one sessions, group therapy, guided fitness and mindfulness practice.
